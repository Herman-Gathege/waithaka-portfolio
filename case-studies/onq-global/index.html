01

Overview

OnQ Global is a global business services and outsourcing partner: contact centre, healthcare revenue cycle management, global labour outsourcing and IT help desk services, alongside AI and automation products such as the Qnity AI Voice Agent, QlearView Analytics and robotic process automation. Since 2013 it has served healthcare, finance, technology, education and retail clients from delivery hubs in Panama, Kenya and India, all aligned to US time zones.

As Digital Experience Specialist I am the developer who builds and maintains onq.global, the company’s WordPress website built on the Avada theme. The site has to explain a wide range of services to buyers in very different industries, so the work covers building and restructuring pages, keeping the site technically healthy and discoverable, and measuring what visitors actually do.

I also lead the current website revamp: auditing the existing site, producing wireframes and presenting the direction to stakeholders, then implementing it in phases.

02

What the work involves

  • Building and maintaining service and product pages — including pages for products such as Qnity AI Voice Agent and QlearView Analytics — with a consistent section structure, responsive layouts and custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript inside Avada.
  • Leading the website revamp, from site audit and wireframe mockups through stakeholder presentations to phased implementation.
  • Running technical SEO: Search Console sitemap submission and indexing audits, fixing redirect, canonical and duplicate-sitemap conflicts, and adding Organization schema in JSON-LD.
  • Setting up and maintaining analytics through Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and Google Site Kit, and using the data to decide what to improve next.
  • Executing on-page SEO — metadata, internal linking and keyword tracking — and optimising the company’s Google Business Profile presence.
  • Keeping pages, navigation and metadata in step as product names and service structure change.
  • Resolving platform issues with hosting, security and plugin vendors, such as Cloudflare rate limiting, image delivery failures and admin access blocks.

03

Approach

  • One page pattern, many services: service and product pages share the same section structure and styling, so a buyer moving from call centre to RPA recognises the layout and new pages are faster to build.
  • Scoped styling: responsive styles live with the component they belong to rather than in global CSS, so a fix on one page cannot quietly break another.
  • Fix indexing before chasing rankings: sitemap, canonical and redirect problems come first, because content that Google cannot index cannot rank.
  • Instrument before you argue: GA4, Tag Manager, Site Kit and Search Console data settle questions about which pages and journeys deserve the next change.
  • Show, then build: wireframes and a clear presentation get stakeholder agreement before implementation begins.

Where it stands

The role is current. The revamp is being delivered in phases, alongside day-to-day page updates, SEO and analytics work.

Because onq.global belongs to OnQ Global, I do not publish its traffic, rankings or conversion data here. The scope of the role is described in full, and the site itself is public at onq.global.

06

Key takeaways

  • A company that sells many services to many industries needs a site structure buyers can navigate quickly; consistency across pages is a feature, not a limitation.
  • Technical SEO is often the highest-return work on an established site: fixing indexing and duplicate signals can matter more than new content.
  • Reliable escalation with vendors is a real engineering skill — the quality of the report usually decides how quickly the issue is fixed.
