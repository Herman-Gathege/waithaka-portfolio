01 The story

From business IT to the front end of the product

My route into engineering started with a BSc. in Business Information Technology at Strathmore University, where I worked through web development, object-oriented programming, database management systems and networking. That grounding still shapes how I work: I think about the system, the data and the person on the other side of the screen — not just the component in front of me.

I moved into software development proper at Moringa School in 2024, and have spent the years since building interfaces in JavaScript, TypeScript, React.js and Vue.js. Today I split my time between an in-house digital experience role at OnQ Global, full-stack delivery for clients at Webloom Tech Kenya, and independent builds where I take a project from design through deployment.

My strongest work sits where design intent meets real constraints: consuming RESTful APIs, managing state with Redux Toolkit and Pinia, keeping SEO-focused single-page applications discoverable, and applying security controls that hold up outside a demo. I prototype with design partners to test feasibility early, and I act as an advocate for the user in review conversations — because decisions grounded in real needs age better.

I work in agile teams, ship through Git-based CI/CD, and review other engineers’ code as carefully as I want mine reviewed.

04 How I work

Four habits that shape the output

01

Start from the user’s task

I act as an advocate for the person using the product, so design and build decisions stay grounded in real needs rather than assumptions.

02

Test design feasibility early

I prototype alongside UI/UX partners so constraints surface while changes are still cheap, and the intent survives the hand-off.

03

Ship through review

Peer code reviews, change control procedures and Git-based pipelines are how work reaches production — not an optional extra at the end.

04

Measure, then improve

Analytics, search data and Core Web Vitals tell me where the experience is falling short, so the next iteration is based on evidence.
